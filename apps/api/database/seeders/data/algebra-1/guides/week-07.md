## What you need to be able to do

- **2.9** Rearrange a formula or an equation with several variables to isolate any one of them, and choose which variable to solve for based on what you need to compute repeatedly.
- **2.10** Rewrite a linear equation in slope-intercept form y = mx + b, read the slope and the y-intercept, interpret them in context and use them to graph the line.
- **2.11** Work with standard form ax + by = c: find intercepts directly, find the slope as −a/b, explain what the coefficients mean in a situation, and move between the two forms.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Slope | The rate of change of y with respect to x: rise over run, (change in y)/(change in x). Written m. |
| y-intercept | The value of y when x = 0; the point (0, b). |
| Slope-intercept form | y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b the y-intercept. |
| Standard form | ax + by = c, with a, b, c constants (usually integers). |
| Coefficient | The number multiplying a variable: in 4x + 3y = 24 the coefficients are 4 and 3. |
| Rate of change | Another name for slope when the variables are quantities: dollars per hour, litres per minute. |

## Solving for any variable (2.9)

The moves never change: undo what was done to the variable, on both sides, until it stands alone.

**Worked example.** The area of a trapezoid is A = (1/2)(b₁ + b₂)h. Solve for b₂.
Multiply both sides by 2: 2A = (b₁ + b₂)h. Divide both sides by h: 2A/h = b₁ + b₂. Subtract b₁: **b₂ = 2A/h − b₁**. With A = 30, h = 5, b₁ = 4: b₂ = 12 − 4 = 8.

**Worked example.** Solve 3x + 2y = 18 for x.
Subtract 2y: 3x = 18 − 2y. Divide by 3: **x = (18 − 2y)/3** = 6 − (2/3)y.

**Which variable?** Solve for the quantity you will compute many times. A loan calculator used by customers who know their monthly payment and want the loan length solves for time; one used by a bank that knows the length and wants the payment solves for payment. Same equation, different isolated letter.

**Try it.** Solve v = u + at for a.
*Answer:* a = (v − u)/t.

## Slope-intercept form (2.10)

Any non-vertical line can be written **y = mx + b**.

- **m, the slope**, is how much y changes when x increases by 1. Positive m rises to the right; negative m falls; m = 0 is horizontal.
- **b, the y-intercept**, is the value of y at x = 0: the point (0, b).

From two points, m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). Through (1, 5) and (3, 11): m = 6/2 = 3. Then 5 = 3(1) + b gives b = 2, so y = 3x + 2.

**Graphing from the form.** Plot (0, b). From there, move right 1 and up m (or down |m| if m is negative); plot; draw the line. For y = −(1/2)x + 4: plot (0, 4), then right 2 and down 1 to (2, 3), and again to (4, 2).

**Rewriting into the form.** From 4x + 3y = 24: subtract 4x to get 3y = −4x + 24; divide by 3 to get y = −(4/3)x + 8. Slope −4/3, y-intercept 8.

**In context.** A plumber's charge C = 45h + 60: slope 45 means $45 per hour; intercept 60 means the charge before any hours are worked, the call-out fee. A draining tank V = 500 − 20t: slope −20 means 20 litres lost per minute; intercept 500 is the starting volume.

**Worked example.** A line passes through (0, −3) and (4, 5). Write its equation.
m = (5 − (−3))/(4 − 0) = 8/4 = 2. The y-intercept is given, −3. **y = 2x − 3.**

**Try it.** Write y = 6 − 1.5x in the form y = mx + b and state the slope and intercept.
*Answer:* y = −1.5x + 6; slope −1.5, y-intercept 6.

## Standard form (2.11)

**ax + by = c** is the natural form when two quantities combine to make a total: 8b + 12f = 960 (balcony and floor tickets), 0.10d + 0.05n = 4.50 (dimes and nickels).

What you can read without rearranging:

- **x-intercept**: set y = 0, x = c/a. For 4x + 3y = 24: 24/4 = 6, point (6, 0).
- **y-intercept**: set x = 0, y = c/b. 24/3 = 8, point (0, 8).
- **Slope**: m = −a/b. For 4x + 3y = 24: −4/3, which matches the rewrite above.

**Meaning of the coefficients.** In 8b + 12f = 960, the 8 and 12 are prices and 960 is the total. The slope −8/12 = −2/3 says that selling 3 more balcony seats means selling 2 fewer floor seats to keep the same total. The intercepts are the "all balcony" and "all floor" possibilities.

**Changing the total.** 8b + 12f = 1,200 has the same slope (−2/3) and different intercepts (150 and 100): a parallel line further from the origin. Changing a coefficient changes the slope.

**Which form when?**

| You want to | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Read the rate of change and starting value | slope-intercept |
| Graph by plotting (0, b) and stepping by m | slope-intercept |
| Model two quantities combining into a total | standard |
| Find both intercepts fast | standard |

**Worked example.** Rewrite y = (2/5)x − 3 in standard form with integer coefficients.
Multiply by 5: 5y = 2x − 15. Move x: −2x + 5y = −15, or **2x − 5y = 15**.

**Try it.** For 6x − 2y = 18, find the intercepts and the slope.
*Answers:* x-intercept (3, 0); y-intercept (0, −9); slope −6/(−2) = 3.

## Common mistakes

- Reading the slope of 4x + 3y = 24 as 4 or as 4/3; it is −a/b = −4/3.
- Dividing only part of the right side when isolating y: from 3y = −4x + 24 writing y = −4x + 8.
- Confusing the y-intercept b with the point: b = 8 means the point (0, 8), not (8, 0).
- Computing slope as run over rise, or subtracting coordinates in different orders in the numerator and denominator.
- Treating "6 − 1.5x" as slope 6: the slope is the coefficient of x, −1.5.

## Self-check

1. Solve 5x − 4y = 40 for y and state the slope.
2. Find both intercepts of 5x − 4y = 40.
3. A line passes through (2, 1) and (6, 9). Write its equation.
4. In C = 0.15m + 20 for a phone bill, interpret 0.15 and 20.
5. Write y = −3x + 7 in standard form.

*Answers:* (1) y = (5/4)x − 10; slope 5/4. (2) (8, 0) and (0, −10). (3) m = 8/4 = 2; 1 = 2(2) + b gives b = −3; y = 2x − 3. (4) $0.15 per minute (or per unit m measures); $20 fixed monthly charge. (5) 3x + y = 7.
