# Attribution

Every literary text quoted in this directory, in study guides, unit
summaries, flashcards and test items, is in the public domain in the United
States (first published in 1930 or earlier; translations likewise). Each
quotation was checked word for word against the edition listed below,
usually the Project Gutenberg text, and is kept short: excerpts of a few
hundred words of prose or a single poem. Where an edition's spelling or
punctuation differs from the familiar modern text, the quotation follows
the edition cited.

All commentary, worked examples, flashcards, questions, answer rationales,
model answers and scoring guides were written for this course. The course
is organised around the College Board AP English Literature and Composition
Course and Exam Description: its nine units, its six big ideas and its
skills (1.A ... 7.E, quoted verbatim in `course.yaml`), and the exam format
and 6-point essay rubric it describes. AP is a trademark registered by the
College Board, which is not affiliated with and does not endorse this course.
No College Board questions are reproduced. Links to the Folger Shakespeare
Library and the Poetry Foundation point to reading editions only; Hamlet is
quoted from Project Gutenberg, not from the Folger text.

`Database\Seeders\ApLiterature\Lint` enforces the public-domain cutoff on
every week's `texts` and every item's `source`.
