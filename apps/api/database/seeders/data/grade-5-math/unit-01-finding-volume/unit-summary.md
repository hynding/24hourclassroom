# Unit 1 · Finding Volume · Summary

**Weeks:** 1 Volume: Filling Space With Unit Cubes · 2 Volume Formulas and Expressions · 3 Volume of Composite Solids
**Standards:** 5.MD.C.3 · 5.MD.C.4 · 5.MD.C.5 · 5.OA.A.2 · **Built from:** IM K–5 Math Grade 5, Unit 1, in our own words

## The big ideas

1. **Volume is space.** The volume of a solid figure is the amount of space it takes up. We measure it by filling the solid with **unit cubes**, with no gaps and no overlaps. Each unit cube is 1 **cubic unit**.
2. **Count every cube.** A picture shows only the front, top and one side. The cubes behind and under them count too.
3. **Layers make counting fast.** Cubes in one layer × number of layers. A prism 4 cubes long, 3 wide and 5 high has 12 cubes in a layer and 5 layers: 60 cubic units.
4. **Two formulas.** V = l × w × h and V = B × h, where B is the area of the base. They say the same thing: length × width is the base, and the height counts layers.
5. **Any grouping works.** (6 × 2) × 5 = 6 × (2 × 5) = 60. Grouping factors differently does not change the product.
6. **Volumes add.** A composite solid made of prisms that do not overlap has a volume equal to the sum of its parts.
7. **Expressions with parentheses.** Parentheses show what to do first. 4 × (250 + 30) is 4 times as large as 250 + 30, which we can say without working it out.

## Strategies with an example

| Strategy | When to use it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Count one layer, then the layers | A prism made of cubes | 3 rows of 6 = 18 in a layer; 4 layers = 72 |
| Count front slices | A staircase or step of cubes | Front 3 + 2 + 1 = 6; 3 deep = 18 |
| Multiply the three edges | A box with labeled edges | 8 cm × 5 cm × 3 cm = 120 cubic cm |
| Base area × height | The base area is given | 45 square ft × 4 ft = 180 cubic ft |
| Choose an easy grouping | Mental math | 25 × 6 × 4 = (25 × 4) × 6 = 600 |
| Divide by the base area | A missing height | 96 ÷ (4 × 6) = 4 |
| Split and add | A composite solid | (3 × 2 × 6) + (5 × 2 × 2) = 36 + 20 = 56 |
| Split another way | Checking a composite answer | (8 × 2 × 2) + (3 × 2 × 4) = 32 + 24 = 56 |
| Big box minus the missing piece | A box with a corner cut out | 96 − 16 = 80 |
| Parentheses for "first" | Writing an expression from words | Add 8 and 2, then times 5: (8 + 2) × 5 |

## Words to know

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Volume | The amount of space a solid figure takes up. |
| Unit cube | A cube whose edges are each 1 unit long. |
| Cubic unit | The space inside one unit cube, such as a cubic centimeter. |
| Rectangular prism | A box shape with 6 rectangle faces. |
| Layer | One flat level of cubes in a prism. |
| Base and base area | The face a prism stands on, and its area, length × width. |
| Height | How tall a prism is: the number of layers. |
| Composite solid | A solid made of two or more prisms put together. |
| Expression | Numbers and operations with no equal sign. |
| Parentheses | Marks ( ) that group the part to do first. |

## Watch out for

- **Counting only the cubes you can see.** A 3 by 3 by 3 cube shows 19 cubes but has 27.
- **Adding the edges.** 4 + 3 + 2 is a length. The volume of a 4 by 3 by 2 box is 4 × 3 × 2 = 24 cubic units.
- **Stopping at one layer.** Always ask how many layers there are.
- **Wrong units.** Volume is in cubic units. Square units measure area.
- **Overlapping parts.** In a composite solid, make each part its own space, so nothing is counted twice.
- **Using the wrong height.** In a composite solid the top part's height is often a difference, like 7 − 3. For water in a tank, use the water's height.
- **Multiplying volumes.** Parts of 24 and 12 cubic feet make 24 + 12 = 36, not 24 × 12.

## Practice set

1. A prism is 5 cubes long, 2 cubes wide and 3 cubes high. What is its volume?
2. Find the volume of a box 9 in by 4 in by 2 in.
3. A box has a base area of 30 square meters and a height of 6 meters. What is its volume?
4. A box has a volume of 56 cubic centimeters and a base 2 cm by 4 cm. How tall is it?
5. Show two groupings for 3 × 5 × 4 and their products.
6. A composite solid is made of a box 6 × 3 × 2 and a box 2 × 3 × 4 that do not overlap. What is its volume?
7. Write "add 15 and 5, then multiply by 3" as an expression. How does its value compare with 15 + 5?

*Answers:* (1) 10 cubes in a layer, 3 layers: 30 cubic units. (2) 9 × 4 × 2 = 72 cubic inches. (3) 30 × 6 = 180 cubic meters. (4) The base is 8 square centimeters and 8 × 7 = 56, so 7 cm. (5) (3 × 5) × 4 = 15 × 4 = 60 and 3 × (5 × 4) = 3 × 20 = 60. (6) 36 + 24 = 60 cubic units. (7) (15 + 5) × 3; it is 3 times as large as 15 + 5.
