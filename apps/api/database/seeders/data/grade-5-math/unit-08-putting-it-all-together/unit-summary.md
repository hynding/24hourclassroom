## The big ideas

1. **Place value runs the standard algorithm.** Multiply by the ones digit, then by the tens digit (with a 0 in the ones place), then add the rows.
2. **Division takes out groups of the divisor.** Take out big, easy chunks, add them, and keep any remainder smaller than the divisor.
3. **Volume counts cubes in layers.** V = l × w × h = B × h. A solid made of two prisms is split into parts that do not overlap, and the volumes are added.
4. **Fractions need same-size pieces.** Rename fractions with a common denominator before you add or subtract.
5. **Decimals follow place value too.** Line up the places to add and subtract. Tenths times tenths make hundredths.
6. **Plans need clear steps.** Ordered pairs say exactly where a point is, and expressions record a calculation before you do it.

## Strategies at a glance

| Strategy | When to use it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Standard algorithm | Multi-digit times multi-digit | 342 × 57 = 2,394 + 17,100 = 19,494 |
| Estimate first | Before any big calculation | 389 × 52 is about 400 × 50 = 20,000 |
| Partial quotients | Dividing by a two-digit number | 3,312 ÷ 24 = 100 + 30 + 8 = 138 |
| Use the remainder | Division in a story | 230 students, buses of 48: 4 R 38, so 5 buses |
| Layers of cubes | Volume of a box | 8 × 5 × 6: 40 cubes in a layer, 6 layers, 240 |
| Split and add | A solid made of two prisms | 120 + 80 = 200 cubic feet |
| Divide the volume | A missing edge | 360 ÷ (9 × 8) = 5 inches |
| Common denominator | Thirds and fourths, and so on | 2/3 + 1/4 = 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12 |
| Trade a whole | Subtracting mixed numbers | 3 3/6 − 1 4/6 = 2 9/6 − 1 4/6 = 1 5/6 |
| Line up places | Adding and subtracting decimals | 6.00 − 2.45 = 3.55 |
| Think in tenths | Dividing decimals | 9.6 ÷ 0.4 = 96 tenths ÷ 4 tenths = 24 |
| Across, then up | Plotting and reading points | (4, 8) is 4 right and 8 up |
| Subtract coordinates | Distance along a grid line | (2, 3) to (7, 3) is 7 − 2 = 5 units |

## Words to know

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Standard algorithm | A way to multiply in columns, one row for each digit. |
| Divisor | The number you divide by. |
| Quotient | The answer to a division. |
| Volume | The space inside a solid, counted in cubic units. |
| Common denominator | A denominator two fractions can share. |
| Equivalent fractions | Fractions that name the same amount. |
| Origin | The point (0, 0), where the axes meet. |
| Expression | Numbers and signs that stand for an amount, like 2 × (5 + 3). |

## Watch out for

- **The missing 0.** In 342 × 57, the second row is 342 × 50 = 17,100, not 1,710.
- **A remainder that is too big.** 1,536 ÷ 32 = 46 R 64 is not finished: 64 holds two more 32s.
- **Ignoring the question.** 4 R 38 buses means 5 buses, but 41 R 16 cases means 41 full cases.
- **Counting a corner twice.** The two prisms you add must not overlap.
- **Adding denominators.** 1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6, not 2/5.
- **Decimal places in products.** 0.3 × 0.2 is 0.06, not 0.6.
- **Switching coordinates.** (3, 7) and (7, 3) are different points.

## Practice set

1. 529 × 64
2. 2,856 ÷ 34
3. A box is 9 cm by 6 cm by 5 cm. What is its volume?
4. A solid is made of a 4 by 3 by 2 prism and a 2 by 3 by 5 prism. What is its volume?
5. 5/6 + 3/8
6. 4 1/5 − 2 1/2
7. 1.8 × 0.5
8. How far apart are (4, 2) and (4, 9)?
9. Write an expression for "add 18 and 7, then divide by 5." What is its value?

*Answers:* (1) 2,116 + 31,740 = 33,856. (2) 34 × 80 = 2,720, leaving 136; 34 × 4 = 136; so 84. (3) 270 cubic centimeters. (4) 24 + 30 = 54 cubic units. (5) 20/24 + 9/24 = 29/24 = 1 5/24. (6) 4 2/10 − 2 5/10 = 3 12/10 − 2 5/10 = 1 7/10. (7) 0.9. (8) 9 − 2 = 7 units. (9) (18 + 7) ÷ 5, which is 25 ÷ 5 = 5.
