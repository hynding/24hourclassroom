# Unit 2 · Fractions as Quotients and Fraction Multiplication · Summary

**Weeks:** 4 Fractions as Quotients: Sharing Equally · 5 Fractions of Whole Numbers · 6 Area of Rectangles With Fraction Side Lengths · 7 Mixed Numbers and Real-World Multiplication
**Standards:** 5.NF.B.3 · 5.NF.B.4 · 5.NF.B.6 · **Built from:** IM K–5 Math Grade 5, Unit 2, in our own words

## The big ideas

1. **A fraction is a division.** 3/4 means 3 ÷ 4. When 4 people share 3 granola bars equally, each gets 3/4 of a bar. The amount being shared is the numerator and the number of people sharing is the denominator.
2. **Shares can be more than 1.** 9 ÷ 2 = 9/2, which is between 4 and 5. A remainder can be shared too: 9 ÷ 2 = 4 R 1, and the leftover 1 split 2 ways gives 4 1/2.
3. **A fraction of a number is a multiplication.** 3/4 of 12 is 3/4 × 12. Divide by the denominator to find one part (12 ÷ 4 = 3), then multiply by the numerator (3 × 3 = 9).
4. **The answer does not have to be a whole number.** 2/3 × 5 = (2 × 5)/3 = 10/3, which is 3 1/3.
5. **Area works with fraction sides.** Cut a unit square into equal pieces. A rectangle 2/3 by 4/5 covers 2 × 4 = 8 of the 3 × 5 = 15 pieces, so its area is 8/15 square unit. Multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators.
6. **Mixed numbers can be split or rewritten.** 3 × 2 1/4 = 6 + 3/4. For mixed × mixed, use an area model of 4 pieces or write both as fractions: 2 1/2 × 1 1/2 = 5/2 × 3/2 = 15/4 = 3 3/4.

## Strategies with an example

| Strategy | When to use it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Share each thing into equal parts | A sharing story | 2 pizzas for 5 people: 1/5 of each pizza, so 2/5 each |
| Write the division as a fraction | Any division | 7 ÷ 3 = 7/3 |
| Find the wholes on either side | Which two whole numbers is it between? | 17/5: 15/5 = 3 and 20/5 = 4 |
| Share the remainder | Turning a remainder into a fraction | 13 ÷ 4 = 3 R 1 = 3 1/4 |
| Divide, then multiply | A fraction of a whole number | 3/8 of 24: 24 ÷ 8 = 3, 3 × 3 = 9 |
| Multiply, then divide | A product that is not a whole number | 3/4 × 7 = 21/4 |
| Tape diagram | Seeing the equal parts | 30 in 5 parts of 6; 2 parts is 12 |
| Tile a unit square | Fraction × fraction area | 1/3 × 1/4 = 1/12 |
| Multiply tops and bottoms | Any fraction-side rectangle | 3/4 × 2/5 = 6/20 |
| Partial products | Whole number × mixed number | 4 × 2 1/3 = 8 + 4/3 = 9 1/3 |
| Area model of 4 pieces | Mixed × mixed | 1 1/2 × 4 1/2 = 4 + 1/2 + 2 + 1/4 = 6 3/4 |
| Estimate with whole numbers | Checking an answer | 4 3/4 × 2 1/8 is about 5 × 2 = 10 |

## Words to know

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Quotient | The answer to a division. In 3 ÷ 4 = 3/4 it is 3/4. |
| Numerator, denominator | The top and bottom numbers of a fraction. |
| Tape diagram | A bar cut into equal parts to show a number and its parts. |
| Unit square | A square with sides 1 unit long and area 1 square unit. |
| Area | The number of square units that cover a shape. |
| Mixed number | A whole number and a fraction, like 2 1/4. |
| Partial products | The smaller products you add up after splitting a factor into parts. |

## Watch out for

- **Flipping a sharing fraction.** 3 bars shared by 4 friends is 3/4, not 4/3. Four friends cannot each get more than one of 3 bars.
- **Giving the number of pieces instead of the share.** If 3 bars are each cut into 4 pieces, each friend gets 3 pieces, which is 3/4 of a bar.
- **Multiplying the denominator by a whole number.** 2/3 × 12 is 24/3 = 8, not 24/36.
- **Stopping after the divide step.** 12 ÷ 3 = 4 is only 1/3 of 12. Two thirds is 8.
- **Adding instead of multiplying sides.** A rectangle 1/2 by 1/3 has area 1/6, not 2/5.
- **Adding the denominators.** A unit square cut into 3 rows and 4 columns has 12 pieces, not 7.
- **Leaving out pieces of the area model.** 2 1/2 × 3 1/2 is not 6 1/4. Include 2 × 1/2 and 1/2 × 3 too: the answer is 8 3/4.

## Practice set

1. Write 5 ÷ 9 as a fraction.
2. Six children share 8 apples equally. How much does each child get, and between which two whole numbers is it?
3. Write 23 ÷ 4 as a mixed number.
4. What is 2/5 of 35?
5. What is 3/4 × 6? Write it as a fraction and as a mixed number.
6. A unit square is cut into 2 rows and 7 columns. What is the area of one piece?
7. What is the area of a rectangle 5/6 meter by 1/2 meter?
8. What is 3 × 2 2/5?
9. What is the area of a rectangle 2 1/2 feet by 2 1/2 feet?
10. A recipe uses 1 1/4 cups of juice. How much is needed for half the recipe?

*Answers:* (1) 5/9. (2) 8 ÷ 6 = 8/6 of an apple, between 1 and 2 (it is 1 2/6). (3) 5 3/4. (4) 35 ÷ 5 = 7, and 2 × 7 = 14. (5) 18/4 = 4 2/4, which is 4 1/2. (6) 1/14 square unit. (7) 5/12 square meter. (8) 6 + 6/5 = 7 1/5. (9) 25/4 = 6 1/4 square feet. (10) Half of 1 is 1/2 and half of 1/4 is 1/8, so 1/2 × 5/4 = 5/8 cup.
