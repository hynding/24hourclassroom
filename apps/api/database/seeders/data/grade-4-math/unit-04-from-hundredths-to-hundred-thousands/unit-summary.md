## The big ideas

1. **Place value works in both directions.** Each place to the left is worth 10 times as much, and each place to the right is worth one tenth as much. That gives us tenths and hundredths after the decimal point, and ten thousands, hundred thousands and a million on the left.
2. **Decimals are fractions with 10 or 100 on the bottom.** 0.7 = 7/10 and 0.07 = 7/100. A zero at the end does not change a decimal: 0.7 = 0.70.
3. **Compare from the biggest place.** For whole numbers, count the digits first. Then, for whole numbers and decimals alike, compare place by place from the left. The first place that differs decides.
4. **Rounding finds the closest friendly number.** Look at the digit one place to the right of the place you are rounding to: 0 to 4 rounds down, 5 to 9 rounds up.
5. **The standard algorithm is place value in short form.** Add or subtract one place at a time, from the ones. Regroup 10 of a place into 1 of the next; decompose 1 of a place into 10 of the next.
6. **Estimate, then check.** Rounded numbers give a quick answer that tells you if an exact answer makes sense, and addition checks subtraction.

## Strategies with an example

| Strategy | Example |
|---|---|
| Fraction to decimal | 45/100 = 0.45; 3 8/100 = 3.08 |
| Hundredths grid | 0.62 = 6 full columns and 2 more squares |
| Decimals on a number line | 0.4 is the fourth tick when 0 to 1 is split into tenths |
| Comparing decimals in hundredths | 0.4 = 0.40, and 40 hundredths is more than 35, so 0.4 is greater than 0.35 |
| Ten times rule | 500 × 10 = 5,000; 50,000 is 100 times as much as 500 |
| Three forms of a number | 306,048 = 300,000 + 6,000 + 40 + 8 = three hundred six thousand, forty-eight |
| Comparing whole numbers | 86,402 is greater than 86,240 (4 hundreds beats 2 hundreds) |
| Rounding with the digit to the right | 362,519 → nearest ten thousand: the next digit is 2, so 360,000 |
| Adding with regrouping | 4,687 + 2,546 = 7,233 |
| Subtracting across zeros | 7,000 − 2,465: 6 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 10 ones → 4,535 |
| Checking with addition | 6,213 − 2,874 = 3,339, because 3,339 + 2,874 = 6,213 |

## Words to know

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Decimal / decimal point | A number like 2.45; the point separates wholes from parts of one. |
| Tenths / hundredths place | The first / second place after the decimal point. |
| Place value | What a digit is worth because of where it sits. |
| Standard, word, expanded form | 52,307; fifty-two thousand, three hundred seven; 50,000 + 2,000 + 300 + 7. |
| Round | Change a number to the nearest friendly number at a chosen place. |
| Estimate | A close answer made with rounded numbers. |
| Regroup / decompose | Trade 10 of a place for 1 of the next / 1 of a place for 10 of the next. |
| Standard algorithm | The column method of adding or subtracting, one place at a time. |

## Watch out for

- **More digits is not bigger for decimals.** 0.35 is less than 0.4, because 3 tenths is less than 4 tenths.
- **Tenths or hundredths?** 7/100 is 0.07, not 0.7. Use a 0 to hold the tenths place.
- **Zeros in big numbers.** 208,040 is two hundred **eight** thousand, forty. Leaving out a zero makes a very different number.
- **Rounding twice.** Round from the number you started with: 14,480 rounds to 14,000 for the nearest thousand.
- **Forgetting to regroup.** 4,687 + 2,546 is not 6,123. The small 1s carry tens, hundreds and thousands.
- **Smaller from bigger.** In 60,005 − 23,148, the ones are 5 − 8, so decompose first. Taking 5 from 8 instead gives a wrong answer.

## Practice (answers at the end)

1. Write 9/100 and 4 3/10 as decimals.
2. Which is greater, 0.6 or 0.58? Explain in hundredths.
3. What is the value of the 3 in 438,915?
4. Write 607,020 in word form and in expanded form.
5. Round 547,281 to the nearest thousand and to the nearest hundred thousand.
6. 29,486 + 53,729
7. 50,000 − 18,627
8. A farm grew 34,560 apples. It sold 12,875 at a market and 9,400 to a store. How many apples are left?

*Answers:* (1) 0.09 and 4.3. (2) 0.6, because 0.6 = 60 hundredths and 60 is more than 58. (3) 30,000. (4) Six hundred seven thousand, twenty; 600,000 + 7,000 + 20. (5) 547,000 and 500,000. (6) 83,215. (7) 31,373. (8) 34,560 − 12,875 = 21,685, then 21,685 − 9,400 = 12,285 apples.
