## What was adapted and what is original

**Adapted from IM** (structure, not text): the nine Grade 4 units and their
order, the unit titles, the sections within each unit, and the approaches
IM uses to build each idea (scaled graphs, equal groups, arrays, area
diagrams, base-ten blocks, number lines, tape diagrams, fraction strips).

**Original to this course**: every guide, summary, worked example, flashcard,
quiz, unit-check and end-of-year-check item (prompts, numbers, contexts,
options, answers, explanations and scoring notes) and every picture in
`figures/`. No IM activity, practice problem, cool-down or assessment item was
reproduced. Nothing was taken from other curricula, worksheet sites or
released state test items. Pictures are SVG files written for this course,
drawn dark on light, with no scripts, images or links.

IM's Grade 4 Unit 9 is a set of review and extension lessons. This course spreads it over three weeks: fractions (week 29), multi-digit multiplication and division (week 30) and problem solving with measurement, money and time (week 31). Section letters follow the order of those topics in the scope and sequence linked from each footer.
