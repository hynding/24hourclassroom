# Week 15 · Converting to Smaller Units

**Standard:** 4.MD.A.1 Know the relative sizes of measurement units and convert to a smaller unit
**Built from:** IM K–5 Math Grade 4, Unit 5, Section B, in our own words

## What you will learn

- Know how big each **unit** is compared with a smaller unit in the same system.
- **Convert** a measurement from a larger unit to a smaller one by multiplying.
- Fill in a **conversion table** and read a double number line.
- Change mixed measurements such as 3 lb 5 oz into one unit.
- Compare two measurements written in different units.

## Words to know

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Unit | A fixed amount we measure with, such as a meter, a gram or a minute. |
| Convert | Write the same amount in a different unit. |
| Conversion table | A table that lists matching amounts in two units. |
| Length | How long something is: km, m, cm, yd, ft, in. |
| Mass and weight | How heavy something is: kg, g, lb, oz. |
| Liquid volume | How much a container holds: L, mL. |

## The facts to know

| Larger unit | Smaller unit | How many times as big |
|---|---|---|
| 1 kilometer (km) | 1,000 meters (m) | 1,000 |
| 1 meter (m) | 100 centimeters (cm) | 100 |
| 1 kilogram (kg) | 1,000 grams (g) | 1,000 |
| 1 liter (L) | 1,000 milliliters (mL) | 1,000 |
| 1 pound (lb) | 16 ounces (oz) | 16 |
| 1 yard (yd) | 3 feet (ft) | 3 |
| 1 foot (ft) | 12 inches (in) | 12 |
| 1 hour (hr) | 60 minutes (min) | 60 |
| 1 minute (min) | 60 seconds (sec) | 60 |

The word part **kilo** means a thousand: a kilometer is 1,000 meters and a kilogram is 1,000 grams. The word part **milli** shows a very small unit: it takes 1,000 milliliters to fill 1 liter.

## Bigger unit, more of the smaller unit

Think about walking across a room. You might take 8 big steps or 16 small steps. The room did not change. Smaller steps just take more of them.

Units work the same way. When you change to a **smaller** unit, you need **more** of them, so you **multiply** by the fact.

- 8 m = 8 × 100 = 800 cm
- 3 kg = 3 × 1,000 = 3,000 g
- 4 hr = 4 × 60 = 240 min

This is the "times as many" idea from last week. A meter is 100 times as long as a centimeter, so 8 meters is 8 × 100 centimeters.

## Conversion tables and double number lines

A conversion table puts matching amounts side by side. Each row adds one more of the fact.

| Yards | Feet |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 9 |
| 4 | 12 |

You can keep the table going by adding 3 each time, or jump to any row by multiplying: 10 yd = 10 × 3 = 30 ft.

A **double number line** shows the same idea. One line is marked 0, 1, 2, 3 kilometers. The line under it is marked 0, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 meters, with each tick lined up with its match.

## Mixed measurements

Some measurements use two units, such as 2 lb 7 oz. To write it in ounces, convert the pounds and then add the ounces that are left.

**Try it.** A melon weighs 2 lb 7 oz. How many ounces is that? *Answer:* 2 lb = 2 × 16 = 32 oz, and 32 + 7 = 39 oz.

Two steps work too. 3 yd = 9 ft, and 9 ft = 9 × 12 = 108 in.

## Comparing in different units

To compare 5 m and 460 cm, write both in the smaller unit. 5 m = 500 cm, and 500 cm is more than 460 cm. So 5 m is longer, by 500 − 460 = 40 cm.

**Try it.** Which is heavier, 3 kg or 2,800 g? *Answer:* 3 kg = 3,000 g, which is more than 2,800 g, so 3 kg is heavier.

## Common mistakes

- **Using the wrong fact.** A meter is 100 cm, not 10 or 1,000. Say the fact before you multiply.
- **Adding instead of multiplying.** 5 feet is not 5 + 12 = 17 inches. Every foot is 12 inches: 5 × 12 = 60.
- **Comparing numbers without units.** 460 is bigger than 5, but 460 cm is shorter than 5 m.
- **Using 100 for time.** An hour has 60 minutes, not 100.

## For grown-ups at home

Labels in the kitchen are full of units. Ask, "This bottle holds 2 L. How many milliliters is that?" or "The recipe says 1 lb of pasta. How many ounces?" A tape measure with inches on one side and centimeters on the other is great for comparing units. Practice the facts in the table above a few at a time.

## Check yourself

1. 7 m = ____ cm
2. 9 L = ____ mL
3. Finish the table: 1 min = 60 sec, 2 min = ____ sec, 3 min = ____ sec.
4. A puppy weighs 4 lb 3 oz. How many ounces is that?
5. Which is longer, 2 km or 1,900 m?

*Answers:* (1) 700 cm. (2) 9,000 mL. (3) 120 sec and 180 sec. (4) 4 × 16 = 64 oz, and 64 + 3 = 67 oz. (5) 2 km, because 2 km = 2,000 m.
